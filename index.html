The Person Behind the Work

Why I chose
to build
the web.

I got into web development because I kept seeing talented businesses — real people with great products and genuine passion — being invisible online. Not because they didn't deserve an audience, but because they didn't have the right digital presence.

That gap frustrated me. So I decided to close it.

What started as curiosity turned into a craft. I taught myself design and code, worked on projects that challenged me, and slowly built a style that's my own — dark, premium, intentional. Every site I build is the result of genuinely caring about the outcome.

I'm based in Kenya, building for clients who want their digital presence to mean something. If that's you, I'd love to work together.
