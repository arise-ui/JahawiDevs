01 — The Challenge

A school with no
digital presence

Divine Favour Junior Academy had been running for years and built a strong reputation in the community through word-of-mouth. But in 2025, parents search before they visit. Without a website, the school was invisible to families actively looking for quality primary education in the area.

The challenge was layered: it wasn't just about getting online — it was about conveying trust, professionalism, and warmth in equal measure. Parents choosing a school for their child are making an emotional decision, not just a practical one. The digital experience needed to reflect that.

"Other schools in the area had websites. We needed ours to be better, not just present."
02 — The Approach

Design for
parent confidence

The design strategy centered on one question: what does a parent need to see to feel confident enough to book a visit?

The answer shaped every decision: clear navigation to admissions and class information, imagery that conveyed the school environment, and a visual identity that felt authoritative without feeling cold.

—
Warm, institutional colour palette

Deep greens and cream tones that communicate stability and care — familiar signals for an educational context.

—
Clear information architecture

Admissions, class levels, and contact information surfaced prominently. Parents shouldn't have to search for what matters most.

—
Mobile-first layout

Most Kenyan parents browse on phone. Every section was designed and tested on mobile before desktop.

—
Fast load times

Images optimised and code kept lean — essential for users on variable mobile data connections.

The homepage — leading with the school's identity and key information above the fold.

03 — The Build

Clean code,
zero shortcuts

Built with semantic HTML, vanilla CSS, and lightweight JavaScript — no frameworks, no page builders. This keeps the site fast, maintainable, and easy to update without needing a developer every time a class list changes.

Deployed on Firebase Hosting, which gives the school a reliable, globally-distributed infrastructure with SSL included and zero server management overhead.

HTML5 CSS3 JavaScript Firebase Hosting
04 — The Outcome

A presence worth
being proud of

The school launched with a website that reflects the quality of its actual offering — professional, warm, and easy to navigate. New families can now find the school online, understand what it offers, and make contact without a phone call.

"JahawiDevs understood exactly what we needed. The site exceeded every expectation — professional, fast, and beautifully designed."— School Director, Divine Favour Junior Academy
